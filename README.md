# ✦ Dayana's Universe

Welcome to **Dayana's Universe**, my personal creative portfolio! This website is a little digital universe where I share who I am, the skills I'm developing, my projects, and some of my creative work.

🌐 **Live website:** [Visit Dayana's Universe](https://dayanamangandi.github.io/dayanas-universe/)

## ✨ About the Project

Dayana's Universe is a personal portfolio designed to bring together technology and creativity in one place. Its space-inspired interface invites visitors to explore different sections through interactive planets.

The portfolio reflects my interest in web development, digital design, video editing, and continuous learning.

## 🚀 Features

- **About Me:** Learn about my story and my interest in technology.
- **My Skills:** Explore my skills in web development, video editing, and graphic design, along with related projects.
- **My Projects:** Discover websites and digital projects I've worked on.
- **Creative Studio:** Browse a gallery of video projects, Canva designs, and ibis Paint edits.
- **Contact Me:** Find my email and LinkedIn profile.

## 🛠️ Built With

- **HTML5** — website structure and content.
- **CSS3** — layout, visual design, animations, and responsive styling.
- **JavaScript** — interactive navigation and website behavior.
- **Google Fonts** — Orbitron and Poppins typography.
- **GitHub Pages** — website hosting.
- **Cloudinary** — video hosting for selected gallery projects.
